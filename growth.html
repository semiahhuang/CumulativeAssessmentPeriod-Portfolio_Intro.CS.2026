I feel like this project represents an area of personal growth for me because I think it has the most cohesive and aesthetically pleasing design of all my projects. I originally had pretty bare designs for my websites and apps because I was just trying to get the more technical aspects down, so I didn’t really worry about having consistent colors or interesting fonts. However, I feel like I finally felt comfortable enough with the technical aspects to play around with the fonts and colors, and I was able to make them more intentional to align with the content of the website. For example, I made the text for “lady bird” pink because that is the color of the cast she wears in the movie. The hardest area for me to master in this project was working out how to center the images. I originally had used the display/block functions to move them a certain distance from the left, but I soon realized that it would not be centered for the other image in the same way. I got past that obstacle by asking my peers about how to change the settings for different images, but I ultimately ended up just experimenting on my own while I waited for a response and figuring it out.
