BEST WORK

Unit 1 Project

This is a proposal for an app I thought of called MusicMood, which is focused on helping users create playlists. I think this is one of my best projects because of how creative the concept is. Although I did not actually build the app, I think that it is a very interesting idea that I truly wish existed. I found that this work was pretty easy because of how much I recognized the gap of not knowing what music to listen to in my own life. As such, I had a pretty clear idea of what could help fix this issue and what would be helpful. I used knowledge about various inputs, such as information given by the user versus information taken from the location of the app itself. I also feel like this work helped me understand key concepts like if-then processing, match processing, and problem solving that came into play in many other projects.
